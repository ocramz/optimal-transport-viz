The circle

SO(2) is a single axis of the SO(3) rotation ball with its two ends joined. A rotation by φ has trace 2 cos φ, so the signed permutations sit at φ = 0° and 180° (identity coupling) and at ±90° (the swap).

Exact solution

The cost is E(φ) = const + (Δ/2) sin²φ with Δ = C12 + C21 − C11 − C22. The flow is φ̇ = −(Δ/2) sin 2φ, so tan φt = tan φ0 · e−Δt. Here Δ = 8 cos θ.

No traps

The landscape has only valleys and peaks, no saddles. Below 90° the identity is optimal; above 90° the swap is; at exactly 90° every rotation costs the same. Every start except an exact peak reaches the optimal coupling. Local minima that are not optimal need n ≥ 3.

Cost: two points at (±1, 0), targets rotated by θ, squared distances. The flow uses the Cayley retraction with Armijo backtracking, as in the post; on SO(2) a Cayley step is an exact rotation by −2 arctan(hω/2).
